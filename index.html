Mathematics of Creativity

This book explore the mathematical architecture woven into creation: the fine-tuning of the cosmos, the harmony of celestial motion, the geometry of nature, and the remarkable efficiency found throughout living systems. These are not isolated phenomena; together they reveal a creation operating with measure, consistency, and wisdom.

The Divine Way

The Divine Way continues the journey begun in The Pattern, exploring the order, purpose, and wisdom woven throughout creation. From the movement of galaxies to the rhythm of everyday life, the book invites readers to reflect on the harmony found within the universe and recognize the signs of the Creator. Through Qur'anic insights, observations of nature, and thoughtful reflection, it encourages a deeper appreciation of Allah's wisdom and a closer relationship with Him.
